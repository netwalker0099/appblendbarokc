Booking Terms

  • A deposit of 50% of the invoice is required to book an event date. Deposits are non-refundable. Without a deposit, your event is not booked.
  • Deposits made for event dates within 21 days of their booking are subject to applicable rush or administrative fees, pending availability, as determined by The Blend Bar.
  • If necessary, multi-day events are required to cover the cost of one (1) hotel room for product and display storage.

Cancellation Policy

  • Events required to cancel their date must provide a minimum of 14 days' written notice, with deposits applied to a future date up to 6 months in advance.
