About Us

The Blend Bar Experience

At The Blend Bar, we believe fragrance should be as unique as the person wearing it. Our luxury mobile perfumery brings a personalized scent experience directly to your event, where each guest is guided through a curated collection of premium fragrance notes to create a custom perfume that reflects their individual style and personality.

Whether you’re celebrating a wedding, bridal shower, corporate event, birthday, or private gathering, our scent stylists provide an elevated, interactive experience that is equal parts memorable and meaningful. Guests blend their own signature fragrance, bottle it in a beautiful keepsake, and leave with a one-of-a-kind scent they created themselves.

Luxury. Fragrance. Personalized.

Booking Terms

  • A deposit of 50% of the invoice is required to book an event date. Deposits are non-refundable. Without a deposit, your event is not booked.
  • Deposits made for event dates within 21 days of their booking are subject to applicable rush or administrative fees, pending availability, as determined by The Blend Bar.
  • If necessary, multi-day events are required to cover the cost of one (1) hotel room for product and display storage.

Cancellation Policy

  • Events required to cancel their date must provide a minimum of 14 days' written notice, with deposits applied to a future date up to 6 months in advance.
