Privacy Policy

Last updated October 22, 2026

The Blend Bar (“we”, “us”) is a mobile perfumery in Oklahoma City. This policy explains what we collect when you blend a scent with us, book an event, sign in to your customer portal or buy from our website, why we collect it, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. It covers theblendbarokc.com and the bar itself.

What we collect

When you blend with us

Our staff record your name and email address, the scents you like, and the formula of any fragrance you create with us, so you can reorder it later. We ask whether you would like to hear from us about offers and events, and we record your answer and when you gave it.

When you book an event

The booking form asks for your name, email address, phone number, the date of your event and any details you choose to share about it.

When you buy online

When you buy from a shared scent link or from our shop, we receive your email address, your name if you give it, what you ordered and any coupon you use. Payment happens on Square’s secure checkout page: we never see or store your card details. For shop orders, Square collects your shipping address so your order can be sent to you; it is kept on the order in Square rather than on our own systems.

When you sign in to your customer portal

To sign in, you give us your email address and we email you a one-time sign-in link. Signing in keeps you logged in on that browser with a cookie (see Cookies and browser storage).

Technical information

Like any website, our servers receive your IP address and basic browser information when you visit. We use IP addresses briefly to limit repeated checkout and booking attempts, which protects against abuse. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools of any kind.

How we use it

  • To make your fragrance and keep your formula so you can reorder it.
  • To take payment for, fulfil and ship what you buy, and to send receipts.
  • To reply to event enquiries and plan your event.
  • To sign you in to your customer portal.
  • To send you offers and news, only if you have said yes.
  • To keep the records the law requires for sales and tax, and to prevent fraud.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the services that run our business for us, and only what each one needs:

  • Square processes payments, receipts and refunds, and keeps our customer list, including whether you have agreed to hear from us. Square’s own privacy policy applies to the information you give on its checkout page.
  • Google Workspace sends our emails (portal sign-in links and a note when your blend is ready to collect) and may store our encrypted backups.
  • Our team chat receives a short message when a new event enquiry or online order arrives, including the customer’s name and email address, so the right person can follow up.
  • Our hosting provider runs the servers this website and our records live on.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect our customers, staff or business from fraud or harm.

Cookies and browser storage

We use only what the site needs to work. Nothing is used for tracking or advertising, and nothing is set by anyone other than us.

Square’s checkout page, which we send you to for payment, is run by Square and uses its own cookies under Square’s privacy policy. You can clear or block cookies and browser storage in your browser’s settings; if you do, the portal will ask you to sign in again and your bag will be emptied.

How long we keep it

We keep your details and formulas for as long as you are a customer, so you can reorder, and event enquiries for as long as they are useful for planning and follow-up. Sales and payment records are kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires, even if you ask us to delete the rest of your information.

Your choices

  • Marketing: you can say no when we ask, and you can stop hearing from us at any time by using the unsubscribe link in any message or by contacting us.
  • Access, correction and deletion: you can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. We will do so unless we need to keep something by law, such as a sales record, and will tell you if that applies.
  • Cookies and storage: see Cookies and browser storage.

To make a request, contact us as described below. We may ask you to confirm it is you before we act on a request, to protect your information.

Security

Our website and systems use encrypted connections throughout. Staff sign in with a password and a second step, access is limited to the people who need it, changes to records are logged, and any backup kept away from our servers is encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will let you know if a problem affects it.

Children

Our website and online shop are not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page, and for significant changes we will make that clear on this site.

Questions or requests about your information? Send us a direct message on Instagram at @theblendbar.okc.
